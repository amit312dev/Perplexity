Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Perplexity ("we", "our", or "the application") handles information when you use the Perplexity web application.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use the application, we may handle:

  • Account information: username and email address provided during registration.
  • Authentication information: password information stored in hashed form rather than plain text, and authentication/session tokens used to keep you signed in.
  • Chat information: messages, conversation history, and related details you submit or create in the application.
  • Technical information: basic request, error, and diagnostic logs where recorded by the application or its hosting providers.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to create and manage accounts, verify email addresses, authenticate users, provide chat and conversation features, maintain conversation history, secure the application, troubleshoot issues, and operate and improve the service.

3. Email Verification

We use the Google Gmail API to send account verification and other account-related emails. Your email address is used for these communications.

4. Storage and Service Providers

Account and application data may be stored in MongoDB Atlas. The frontend and backend are hosted using Vercel and Render. These providers may process information as needed to host, operate, secure, and deliver the application, subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their independent advertising purposes. Information may be processed by the service providers described above to operate the application, or disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the application and its users.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and conversation information for as long as needed to provide the application and maintain its operation. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account information, contact us using the email address below. We may need to retain certain information where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The application is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
